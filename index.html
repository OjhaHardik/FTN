Hill sites are our everyday work.

Most of our projects sit on slopes. These drawings are standard in our sets, not extras.

  • Contour surveyTotal station survey of levels, trees and boundaries before any line is drawn.
  • Retaining wallsWalls sized for the cut, with drainage behind them.
  • Cut and fillEarthwork planned so the house sits into the slope, not on top of it.
  • Slope drainageStorm water routed around the building and off the site.
  • Sloping roofsTrusses, purlins and sheeting detailed for snow and monsoon.

From plot to handover.

  1. Survey

    Manual or total station survey of the plot, its contours, trees and existing services.

  2. Design

    Concept, layouts and 3D views, revised with you until the plan fits the site and the family.

  3. Detail

    Working drawings for structure, masonry, doors, windows, plumbing, wiring and drainage.

  4. Supervise

    Site visits at key stages, with measurement and bill checks against the drawings.
